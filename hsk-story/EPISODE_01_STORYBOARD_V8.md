# Episode 01 / V8 storyboard — Ngày đầu xa nhà
Status: authored interactive pilot, NOT complete HSK1.

## Narrative spine
1. Arrive in Chengdu. Mother gives Minh An a small notebook and the advice to understand one more person each day.
2. Meet Wang teacher at school gate. Learn to introduce An by name.
3. Xiao Yu becomes first friend; both share a classroom and count three students.
4. NEW ACTIVE EVENT: the notebook goes missing. Inspect two different clues (desk, schoolbag, doorway), choose Xiao Yu or Wang teacher, go to the staff office, say An's name in Mandarin to reclaim the book.
5. Lunch with Liu Nainai: order noodles, select mild/spicy flavor, hear price ten yuan, thank the owner.
6. On the walk home, An thinks about the notebook and whoever helped retrieve it. The scene callback differs by route.
7. The following day brings a three-question classroom assessment (not an official HSK exam).
8. Ending: the recovered diary returns to mother's first words and An's first friend.

## Transfer / scoring design
The staff asks 你叫什么名字？ in a different location after the self-introduction was introduced in scene 1. Accept previously practiced natural forms 我叫安 / 我叫明安 / 我是安. This is a FORMATIVE transfer task: correct independently on first try, correct after mistakes, correct with explicit assistance, and viewing a sample are separate outcomes. Searching scene objects and picking a companion do not award language points. All outcomes eventually return to mandatory scene 4 and the pre-existing school assessment, not a shortcut.
Two unique inspected clues are required; repeated clicking one object does not count twice. A sample answer appears only after two failed attempts. Help, typed draft, route, clues and outcome persist on reload; replay resets while school exam retake preserves them.
Existing numeric scene IDs 0-9 are unchanged. Old V7 saves beyond the event automatically skip the new content rather than lose progress.

## Visual and content direction
Original thin hand-inked muted pastel style, lived-in classroom desk/bag/door, staff desk and tactile notebook details. Separate background, expression sprites and HTML text; do not copy a provided copyrighted reference drawing. V8 still uses existing SVG classroom as placeholder while object interactions use accessible text controls.
A Mandarin teacher and bilingual editor must verify naturalness and Pinyin. The transfer task is not evidence of full HSK1 proficiency or tone ability.

## Continuity law
The notebook is the same object that mother gave An. If Xiao Yu accompanies An, she refers to it on the walk home; if Wang teacher helps, An remembers having sought her guidance. Both routes regain the notebook and preserve all learning objectives. The ending closes the emotional arc without claiming a formal HSK certificate.
