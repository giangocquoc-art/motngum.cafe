# Story and art bible
Current product authority: [docs/CHIM_SE_DIRECTION.md](docs/CHIM_SE_DIRECTION.md). Historical chapter plans below do not expand the current playable scope.

## Chim Sẻ character canon
Minh An/安 is an 11-year-old Vietnamese boy with short tousled black hair, a mustard yellow hoodie and a faded blue-green backpack. He is curious, sometimes clumsy, embarrassed to ask and capable of misunderstanding. Growth happens through actions and relationships rather than a flawless personality.

Sẻ is one pale-blue sparrow with a cream belly. Only An understands its words; everyone else hears birdsong. Sẻ is a character in the world, not an all-knowing answer key: it can hesitate, get curious and guess wrongly. It must not reveal checkpoint answers. Keep exactly one Sẻ in each composed scene.

The evening prologue introduces the notebook and An's first encounter with Sẻ. The next day runs school arrival → friendship/seat choice → counting → lost notebook/recovery → noodle order/taste/payment → walk home. The classroom checkpoints happen the following morning. “Hai quê hương” remains a long-term theme; the product is Chim Sẻ. Some of these intended Sẻ moments still need to be authored in runtime content.
## Story
Aged 11, Vietnamese Minh An arrives in Chengdu with mother. A small notebook holds the first Chinese words and the line "Mỗi ngày, chỉ cần hiểu thêm một người." Wang Laoshi is a supportive teacher, Xiao Yu is the first friend, Liu Nainai owns a familiar food stall. The six-stage HSK 3.0 story roadmap proposes arcs from newcomer to the memories An leaves behind; only the HSK1 pilot is currently playable and later arcs remain unreviewed outlines. Character identities, relationships, ages and facts must stay coherent.
## Narrative law
Generative text can elaborate and respond within scene bounds; major canon events must be scripted. Narrative choices leave authored memories and visible callbacks, then rejoin before required learning objectives. The same objectives and equivalent practice appear on every route. Correctness, mistakes, hints, samples and skips affect learning evidence only, never story route or ending. A school assessment must retrieve learned language in a fresh context, not introduce a new expression. Character growth mirrors language growth.
## Visual bible
Original hand-drawn editorial 2D slice-of-life aesthetic: thin irregular dark ink outlines, muted pastel flat fills, lightly textured paper, imperfect natural perspective, lived-in Chinese interiors, restrained natural anatomy. Palette #FDFBE3 #E2B8AC #F8E1AC #C6CFBE #54676B #723F2B #191723. Avoid 3D, glossy objects, photorealism, chibi, generic colorful anime or generic corporate vectors.
Create original background, transparent character sprites, UI HTML dialogue and animation layers separately. Never copy the reference composition, likenesses or exact art. Content should remain accessible as text rather than Chinese characters baked into artwork. Production requires character model sheets, art asset rights/provenance and reviewed consistency.

## V5 authored choice continuity
Two non-assessed choices: after greeting Xiao Yu, An sits near him or asks teacher Wang for help; after ordering noodles, An chooses fewer chilies or tries a little Chengdu spice. The following scenes receive choice-sensitive narration, but all branches reconverge before the next mandatory language task. Persist these in storyChoices and include memories in An's notebook. Do not alter canon or imply these are HSK exam questions.

## V8: the lost notebook (story canon)
After Wang Laoshi's counting lesson, Minh An notices the mother's notebook is missing. The learner must inspect two distinct clues, then either ask Xiao Yu to accompany An or seek teacher Wang's guidance. Staff at school ask An's name, which An can say in Mandarin using a previously learned expression, and the notebook is returned. This is formative transfer, not an official HSK score. At the walk-home scene the choice has a callback. The episode conclusion returns to mother's initial words. Both routes must reconverge before cafeteria; no optional decision skips a mandatory learning event.

## Chapter 00 prologue — before HSK1
The evening before An's first new school day in Chengdu, mother opens the same notebook she gave An on arrival. She shows pictographic mnemonic shapes, simple numerals and easy combinations of Hanzi. The prologue stays low-stakes and age-appropriate and is optional for learners who already recognize Chinese writing. It foreshadows the diary's later disappearance and recoverability in HSK1. This introductory prologue does not change canonical HSK1 state or grant HSK exam competence. A character-construction joke about women is explicitly checked against historical meaning and critiqued for its period's gender prejudice rather than reinforced.

## Gate5 chronology and meaningful callbacks
Chapter00 takes place on the evening after An and mother have settled in Chengdu, **before the first day at school**. HSK1 first scene is the following morning, with mother arriving near the new school and pointing to the *already gifted* notebook; no second gifting. After the two branch choices, later scenes reflect the pupil/teacher seating route and chili/mild noodles choice through Chinese phrases seen in prior scenes. The episode coda recalls these decisions without altering HSK checkpoint grades or creating new canon beyond this day. Only content already encountered can be used as an educational “echo”; no future spoilers or fake mastery.
